# Game ideas: incremental games for Steam (PC)

All games target **Steam on PC**. No mobile versions.

## What makes an idea a "10/10" here

A game in this list has to pass all six tests:

1. **Understood from a 5-second clip.** A Steam trailer or a YouTube Short shows the whole idea without sound.
2. **The scale keeps exploding.** Every idea starts tiny and ends absurdly big. If the ending fits inside a house, it's too small.
3. **A sense of progression.** New zones, new enemies, a growing upgrade tree and bosses. Not just bigger numbers.
4. **Done in 2–4 hours.** Short enough to finish, long enough to feel worth $3–5.
5. **Cheap art.** Simple shapes or pixel art. The satisfaction comes from effects, sound and scale.
6. **Sells at $2.99–$4.99**, with a free Steam demo for Next Fest to collect wishlists.

Nobody can guarantee a hit. These ideas have the best odds because proven games already use the same core loops. Every name is a placeholder until we check that it isn't already taken.

## My top 3 to test

1. **Deepline.** Version 0.2 exists: three zones, three bosses, an upgrade tree and progress that saves.
2. **Tiny Singularity.** It has the strongest hook for Steam trailers and YouTube.
3. **Ant Empire.** It gives the biggest emotional arc, from one ant to a civilization on Mars.
